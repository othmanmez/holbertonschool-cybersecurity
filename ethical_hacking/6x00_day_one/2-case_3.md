# Case 3 – The Overreach

## 1. What happened

InnovateTech asked Apex Security Partners for a "red team assessment." The client mainly wanted website testing and possibly phishing, but the consulting firm interpreted the term "red team" as including physical intrusion. During the engagement, a consultant entered the company's building while pretending to be a delivery courier and reached a server room. The police were called because the physical test had not been clearly authorized. The engagement was terminated and the company suffered reputational damage.

## 2. What rules, laws, or professional standards were violated

The main problem was that the engagement was not clearly defined.

Apex assumed that the term "red team" automatically authorized physical intrusion. However, the contract did not clearly mention:

* physical testing
* authorized buildings
* physical locations
* physical intrusion methods
* procedures for dealing with law enforcement

The professional problem was the failure to clarify what the client actually meant by "red team."

Even if physical testing is commonly included in some red team engagements, it should not be assumed. The exact activities need to be written in the scope and Rules of Engagement.

## 3. What the correct professional behavior would have been

Apex should have asked the client what they expected from a red team assessment.

The account manager should have clarified whether the client wanted:

* a normal penetration test
* a vulnerability assessment
* a red team exercise
* social engineering
* physical security testing

If physical testing was included, the consultant should have had:

* written authorization
* a list of authorized locations
* approved testing times
* rules for interacting with security staff
* emergency contacts
* a letter explaining the authorization to present to law enforcement

The consultant should not have assumed that physical intrusion was allowed simply because the words "red team" appeared in the contract.

## 4. Clauses that could have prevented this outcome

### Definition of Engagement

"The engagement type and activities included in the engagement must be specifically defined. General terms such as 'red team' do not automatically authorize activities that are not explicitly listed."

### Physical Testing

"Physical security testing is not authorized unless specifically listed in the scope and approved in writing by the client."

### Authorized Locations

"Physical testing is limited to the buildings and locations specifically identified in this agreement."

### Law Enforcement

"The client will provide a written authorization letter for approved physical testing that identifies the testers and confirms the authorization."

### Physical Testing Rules

"The Rules of Engagement must define permitted physical access methods, prohibited actions, testing hours, emergency contacts, and procedures for dealing with security personnel or law enforcement."
