# MediPath Business and Operational Metrics

All figures below are fictional client-supplied engagement data for use in this project.

## Scale

- 400 contracted medical-analysis laboratories.
- 1.9 million active patient profiles.
- Approximately 62,000 samples processed through the workflow each business day.
- Approximately 14 million result documents delivered annually.
- Operations in France, Belgium, and the Netherlands.
- 180 employees.

## Financial context

- Annual recurring revenue: EUR 31.2 million.
- Average annual contract value per laboratory: approximately EUR 78,000.
- Estimated direct operating and service-credit exposure for a core-platform outage: EUR 65,000 to EUR 120,000 per hour, depending on duration and laboratory volume.
- Cyber-insurance deductible: EUR 250,000.
- Existing external incident-response retainer: EUR 180,000 per year.
- A 1% laboratory-client churn scenario represents approximately EUR 312,000 in annual recurring revenue.
- A 5% laboratory-client churn scenario represents approximately EUR 1.56 million in annual recurring revenue.

## Operational context

- Result-delivery delays exceeding two hours trigger executive escalation.
- Manual laboratory reconciliation is possible for limited periods but creates staffing and error costs.
- High-volume export incidents require DPO, legal, security, and laboratory-notification analysis.
- HDS renewal evidence collection is in progress, with the renewal audit planned within the next five months.
- The audit committee has an eight-week budget and governance window after receipt of the final report.

## Quantification guidance

Use ranges rather than false precision. State assumptions for:

- affected laboratory count;
- affected patient or result volume;
- outage duration;
- incident-response and notification scope;
- customer churn;
- service credits;
- regulatory-response scenario;
- remediation cost and timing.

The maximum legal sanction ceiling is not the same as the expected loss. Separate ceiling, plausible scenario, central estimate, and residual uncertainty.
