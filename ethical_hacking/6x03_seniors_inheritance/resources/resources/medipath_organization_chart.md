# MediPath Organization Chart and Responsibility Map

## Executive and governance roles

| Role | Primary responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Chief Executive Officer | Business continuity, strategic risk acceptance, committee decisions |
| Chief Financial Officer | Budget approval, financial modeling, insurance and loss assumptions |
| Chief Information Security Officer | Security program, incident readiness, risk treatment coordination |
| Data Protection Officer | RGPD accountability, health-data processing, notification and export approvals |
| Technical Director | Architecture, engineering delivery, remediation assurance |
| Board Observer | Governance oversight and committee challenge |

## Operational roles

| Role | Primary responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Head of Platform Engineering | Core API, results service, tenant isolation |
| Identity and Access Management Lead | Roles, sessions, service tokens, revocation |
| Integration Engineering Lead | Hospital connectors, webhooks, partner security |
| Site Reliability Engineering Lead | Network controls, gateways, monitoring, availability |
| Support Operations Manager | Support portal, break-glass process, support workforce controls |
| Data Platform Lead | Analytics service, data warehouse, reporting access |
| Product Owner, Diagnostic Workflow | Sample-state rules and care-path workflow design |
| Compliance and HDS Manager | HDS evidence, audit readiness, control traceability |

## Audit committee composition

- CEO — non-technical business and care-delivery lens.
- DPO — regulatory and health-data lens.
- Technical Director — technical feasibility and architecture lens.
- Board Observer — governance and oversight lens.
- CISO — report owner and remediation-budget sponsor.

## Ownership guidance

A stakeholder who is interested in a finding is not automatically its remediation owner. Assign ownership to the role that can authorize and deliver the corrective action, while listing regulatory, governance, and business stakeholders separately where needed.
