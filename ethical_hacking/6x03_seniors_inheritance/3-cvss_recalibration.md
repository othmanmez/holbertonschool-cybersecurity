# Environmental CVSS Recalibration

| Finding | Sarah base (severity) | Environmental (severity) | Delta | Justification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F-01 | 8.1 (High) | 9.5 (Critical) | ↑ | MediPath processes health data and result records are a high-confidence patient-care dependency, so the confidentiality and integrity requirements are materially higher than a generic SaaS base score suggests. The affected results API sits at the center of the diagnostic pathway, and cross-laboratory disclosure would undermine both HDS accountability and patient trust. |
| F-02 | 6.1 (Medium) | 5.4 (Medium) | ↓ | The support portal is protected by MFA and managed workstations, which materially reduces the likelihood of a successful same-origin abuse path in normal operations. The impact remains real, but the client context limits practical exposure relative to the raw base score because the vulnerable surface is not a broad public attack path. |
| F-03 | 8.8 (High) | 9.6 (Critical) | ↑ | The hospital connector token carries a broad cross-tenant scope and sits on a trust boundary used by multiple institutional integrations, which raises the impact of unauthorized use under the MediPath architecture. Because connector credentials are treated as high-value integration secrets and affect a regulated clinical workflow, the environmental severity is higher than the base score. |
| F-04 | 8.2 (High) | 5.9 (Medium) | ↓ | The connector worker does not connect directly to the public internet and instead traverses a managed private integration network with mTLS enforcement, which materially reduces the live exploitability of the disabled certificate validation. The issue remains a meaningful defense-in-depth weakness, but the client context significantly reduces the operational likelihood and impact compared with a generic network exposure model. |
| F-05 | 7.5 (High) | 7.8 (High) | ↑ | The sample-status webhook can alter the diagnostic state of a sample, and the workflow integrity requirement is very high because false status transitions can disrupt care-path processing and downstream result delivery. The private integration network and source IP allowlist reduce exposure, but they do not eliminate the integrity impact of unsigned or unverified partner events. |
| F-06 | 6.5 (Medium) | 7.3 (High) | ↑ | Export retention is a regulated health-data control failure under the HDS and RGPD context, and the discrepancy between documented and observed expiry periods raises accountability risk for the DPO and compliance team. The operational scale of 14 million result documents annually means even a modest retention mismatch can become a material privacy and audit issue. |
| F-07 | 9.1 (Critical) | 7.4 (High) | ↓ | The bulk-export capability is not an unrestricted public function; the client context shows a DPO-controlled emergency workflow with WebAuthn step-up, dual approval, case-specific encryption, and seven-day expiry. This materially reduces the likelihood of abuse in the live environment, even though the underlying export capability remains sensitive because it handles high-volume patient data. |
| F-08 | 5.3 (Medium) | 4.8 (Medium) | ↓ | The account-enumeration issue is real, but the environment uses SSO and MFA for most laboratory users and the practical effect is reduced by the narrower role model and operational monitoring. The issue still deserves remediation, but the raw base score overstates the live exposure in MediPath's control environment. |
| F-09 | 7.2 (High) | 8.1 (High) | ↑ | The stale administrative session after role downgrade is significant because administrative and support roles are central to the trust model and the authorization cache is a shared service dependency. In a health-data platform, a lingering privileged session creates both operational risk and audit-relevance, so the environmental impact is slightly elevated over the generic base assessment. |
| F-10 | 8.0 (High) | 8.9 (High) | ↑ | The analytics service is logically partitioned by laboratory but it trusts a caller-supplied tenant header, which makes cross-laboratory data exposure possible from an internal application path. Because the service sits adjacent to result and workflow data and shares a trust boundary with the application network, the environmental severity remains elevated even with gateway controls in place. |
