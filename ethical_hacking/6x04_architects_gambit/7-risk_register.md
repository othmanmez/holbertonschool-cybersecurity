# Engagement Risk Register, Nordstrøm Power Group

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Trigger | Owner |
| ---- | ---------- | ------ | ---------- | ------- | ----- |
| Scope creep through broad stakeholder requests across five subsidiaries | Medium | High | Maintain a formal scope-change gate; tie every additional request to decision value, cost impact, and time impact; require written approval before extending test perimeter or staffing; keep a documented exclusions list and a decision log | Any new request expands the perimeter beyond the approved scope, adds a new entity, or changes the planned technical depth without approval | Engagement Lead |
| Conflict of interest from overlapping prior work by another firm or prior auditor | Medium | High | Establish a conflict review before fieldwork; document prior engagements, third-party dependencies, and evidence provenance; keep the assessment independent of prior reports unless explicitly cleared; if overlap is significant, narrow the scope or escalate to partner review | Material overlap is identified with another advisory or assurance provider, or a prior report is used as evidence without an approved dependency check | Principal Consultant / Partner |
| Board deadline compression and schedule slippage | Medium | High | Build a milestone-based plan with float for local coordination; sequence high-value work first; daily internal status review; define escalation path to re-prioritise or defer lower-value tasks if the schedule slips | Any milestone misses the planned window by more than five working days, or critical dependency on a subsidiary is delayed beyond agreed dates | Project Manager / Engagement Lead |
| OT incident during testing in live industrial or process-adjacent environments | Low-Medium | Critical | Restrict testing to approved, low-risk, non-destructive surfaces; define technical stop conditions in advance; require engineering approval for any production-adjacent validation; keep OT specialist and local operations representative in the loop | Any unexpected system behaviour, process interruption, unexpected dependency activation, or engineering alarm during agreed testing | OT Security Specialist |
| Subsidiary cooperation failure where local CISOs resist group orchestration | Medium | High | Establish clear sponsor alignment at the holding level; require local governance sign-off for each subsidiary plan; map stakeholder interests early; use holding-level sponsorship to reduce local friction; maintain a local escalation route for access or evidence constraints | A subsidiary refuses interviews, delays evidence access, or insists on separate approval paths that block agreed milestones | Engagement Lead |
| Legal risk across multiple jurisdictions due to differing regulatory interpretations and evidence handling | Medium | High | Define a jurisdiction-aware operating model before fieldwork; align evidence handling, access approvals, and reporting obligations with local counsel or NIS2 specialist; limit cross-border data movement where required; document assumptions and exceptions in writing | A local entity introduces a new legal restriction, a regulator or external advisor is engaged, or a data-handling boundary is questioned by a local stakeholder | Regulatory / NIS2 Consultant |
| Unknown dependency on legacy systems in the German integration | Medium | High | Treat the German acquisition as a separate validation track; require independent review of legacy-to-new trust boundaries; document live and transitional dependencies explicitly; defer deep testing of uncertain systems until evidence is validated | A migration dependency or inherited system is discovered without clear ownership, or a prior audit result is used to justify trust assumptions without validation | German-language Integration Analyst |
| Misalignment between commercial pressure and technical reality | Medium | Medium-High | Keep the commercial committee informed through milestone-based evidence; require a documented rationale for every change; avoid hidden assumptions in pricing or staffing; escalate if the scope no longer matches the resource or time profile | A new request threatens the agreed cost envelope, staffing model, or delivery date without a formal change approval | Principal Consultant |
