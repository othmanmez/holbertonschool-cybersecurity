# Clarification Questions, Nordstrøm Power Group

## Scope
1. Which exact entities and asset classes are in scope for the initial 90-day assessment: the holding company only, each operating subsidiary individually, or all production, transmission, trading, and digital assets across the group?
   _Informs: the decision to adopt a group-level risk model with phased subsidiary deep dives versus a single broad enterprise assessment_

2. Which parts of the environment are already under formal remediation or prior assurance review, and which parts remain effectively “unknown to the current governance stack”?
   _Informs: the decision to rely on existing assurance evidence versus allocate independent validation effort to specific subsidiaries_

3. For the German subsidiary, what is the current state of the acquisition integration: complete, partial, or still in parallel operation with legacy controls and data flows?
   _Informs: the decision to sequence the German entity as an independent validation track, defer detailed testing, or treat the acquisition as a separate conflict-sensitive assurance domain_

## Timeline
4. Is the board deadline a hard commitment for a final three-year plan, or is the 90-day window simply the point at which the board expects a directional recommendation and an evidence-backed roadmap?
   _Informs: the decision to structure the engagement around a strategic baseline and phased execution plan rather than a full-state maturity assessment in one pass_

5. Are there any internal governance or capital decisions expected to be tied to the plan within the same quarter, such as funding approvals, vendor changes, or architecture refreshes?
   _Informs: the decision to prioritise board-decision support deliverables and critical dependency mapping ahead of lower-value technical detail_

## Budget
6. Is the 800,000 EUR retainer a fixed ceiling, a target range, or a placeholder that can expand only if scope is formally re-baselined?
   _Informs: the decision to define a phased delivery model with explicit assumptions, exclusions, and change-control triggers_

## Constraints
7. Are there any restrictions on facility access, OT testing, third-party vendor interviews, or cross-border data movement across the five subsidiaries and their jurisdictional boundaries?
   _Informs: the decision to design a fieldwork model that is legally and operationally feasible without creating unacceptable regulatory or contractual exposure_

8. Which regulators, national CSIRT contacts, insurers, or external assurance parties are already involved in the group or relevant subsidiaries, and which of those relationships will shape the engagement's evidence and reporting requirements?
   _Informs: the decision to include regulator-aware reporting, regulator-sensitive evidence handling, and jurisdiction-specific communication planning_

## Board expectations
9. What decision is the board actually trying to make with this plan: regulatory readiness, cyber investment prioritisation, risk reduction sequencing, or a post-acquisition control integration case?
   _Informs: the decision to tailor the deliverables towards the board's actual decision lane rather than producing a generic cyber maturity package_

10. Does the board expect a deliverable that is already aligned to an external assurance or audit cycle, or is the plan intended primarily to inform internal strategy and funding before any formal assurance review begins?
   _Informs: the decision to shape evidence quality, governance mapping, and recommended control narratives around the downstream assurance use case_

## Technical preferences
11. Does Nordstrøm want a pure advisory assessment, a controlled technical validation of agreed perimeters, or a combined architecture-plus-assurance model that can support both board decisions and operational remediation planning?
   _Informs: the decision to scope PTES phases, test perimeter boundaries, and reporting depth according to a practical value-based architecture rather than a maximalist security inventory_
